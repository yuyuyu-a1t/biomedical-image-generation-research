# Research protocol

- Topic: major methodological schools of biomedical image generation
- Date: 2026-07-27
- Scope: radiology, pathology, microscopy and cross-modality medical image synthesis; excludes molecular graph/protein generation unless an image is the generated object.
- Evidence priority: peer-reviewed medical-imaging and computer-vision venues/journals; influential preprints are explicitly marked.
- Search concepts:
  1. medical/biomedical image synthesis + generative adversarial network/GAN
  2. medical image synthesis + variational autoencoder/VAE
  3. medical image generation + diffusion/score-based model
  4. medical image synthesis + autoregressive/normalizing flow
  5. text-to-medical-image/foundation model/latent diffusion
- Expert perspectives:
  1. Imaging-methods researcher: model taxonomy, objectives and conditioning mechanisms.
  2. Clinical-translational researcher: fidelity, utility, pathology preservation and deployment.
  3. Evaluation/statistics specialist: metrics, privacy, memorization and distribution shift.
  4. Data/modality specialist: CT, MRI, X-ray, pathology, microscopy and 3D generation.
