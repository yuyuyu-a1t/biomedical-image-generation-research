# Phase 3 paper selection

Nine full-text papers were selected to maximize methodological and clinical coverage:

1. [@nie2017medical] — early paired 3D medical GAN; establishes the adversarial translation lineage.
2. [@cohen2018distribution] — safety counterexample showing that target-distribution matching can add/remove tumors.
3. [@pinaya2022unsupervised] — VQ-VAE plus autoregressive Transformers; represents the discrete-token/explicit-likelihood branch.
4. [@wolleb2022diffusion] — early DDIM-based disease-to-healthy translation and anomaly mapping.
5. [@pinaya2022brain] — early conditional 3D latent diffusion at UK Biobank scale.
6. [@khader2023denoising] — multi-organ/modality 3D latent diffusion with radiologist and downstream-task evaluation.
7. [@zhan2024medm] — unified text, CT, MRI and X-ray generation; represents multimodal foundation-style integration.
8. [@guo2025maisi] — scalable, controllable, high-resolution 3D CT generation with a foundation compression model and ControlNet.
9. [@yeganeh2025latent] — counterfactual generation and natural-to-medical diffusion adaptation at the 2025 frontier.

Selection balances foundational impact, recency, model diversity, 2D/3D imaging, radiology/pathology-adjacent concerns, and direct clinical-safety evidence.
